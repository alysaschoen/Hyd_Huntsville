Why Huntsville

A deep bench, not a single pocket

In most metros, premium households cluster in one small wedge of town. Huntsville is different: strong ZIPs ring the city on nearly every side, which is what makes several territories possible.

Viable ZIP codes
19 of 35

More than half the ZIP codes in the two-county metro clear a median household income of $85K. The typical metro ZIP earns about $90K.

Residents, compact option

In the compact option: five draft territories, each sized around 50,000 to 75,000 residents, with room left over for expansion.

Homeowner market
72–90%

Single-family homes make up the large majority of housing in every draft territory, the driveways and backyards a mobile van is built for.

Growth
#1

Huntsville became Alabama's largest city in the 2020 Census, anchored by Redstone Arsenal and Cummings Research Park, with Madison, Harvest and Limestone County growing around it.

How to read this

The mobile-grooming lens

A mobile grooming van is a different business from a salon. These six measures are what we used to screen the metro and shape the draft territories.

01 · Route density

Route density

Occupied single-family homes per square mile

A van earns while it grooms, not while it drives. The more driveways packed into each square mile, the more appointments fit into a day.

02 · Spending power

Spending power

Median household income

Mobile grooming is a premium, come-to-you service. Household income is the clearest predictor of who books and how often.

03 · Home value

Home value

Median value of owner-occupied homes

Established homeowners stay put and treat convenience services as routine, building a steady recurring book.

04 · Housing fit

Housing fit

Share of single-unit homes

A van needs a driveway or curb and a homeowner at the door. Apartment-heavy areas are harder to serve and slower to convert.

05 · Recurring fit

Recurring fit

Married-couple households

Families in single-family homes are the ideal repeat client: busy schedules, backyards and dogs that need grooming every 4 to 8 weeks.

06 · Compactness

Compactness

Territory span & average distance between stops

Every territory's ZIPs touch, and shape matters as much as size. A compact territory keeps stops close together and windshield time down.

Building the territories

Three things to balance

There is more than one right way to draw Huntsville. Every option trades off the same three things, and the best answer depends on which matters most.

Profit potential

The right households

Income, home value and single-family share decide who books and how often. The more strong ZIPs a territory holds, the deeper its client base.

Windshield time

Stops close together

A van earns while it grooms, not while it drives. Compact, round territories with dense neighborhoods fit more appointments into a day.

Search optimization

A name people search

Pet owners search by the place they live: "mobile dog grooming Madison" or "Hampton Cove." Territories built around one recognized name are easier to rank for and market.

Methodology & sources

Population, households, housing units, housing mix, income, home values, married-couple households and work-from-home rates come from the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey 2020–2024 5-year estimates at the ZIP Code Tabulation Area level, the most recent release available for ZIP codes. Land area is from the 2024 Census Gazetteer; ZIP boundaries and adjacency are from 2020 Census ZCTA boundaries. Territory totals sum each ZIP; percentages are weighted by housing units, income by households, and home value by occupied single-family homes. Because the Census publishes medians, combined income and home-value figures are close estimates rather than exact territory medians.

Territory configurations shown are drafts and are still being evaluated through Hydrodog's Territory Viability Matrix. Each scorecard measure is the territory's percentile against the 35 metro ZIP codes. Compactness is measured from each territory's ZIP boundaries as its longest span and the average straight-line distance between two random points inside it; it compares territories but is not a drive-time estimate. Place names reflect common local usage and may differ from mailing names. Territory boundaries are a working draft.

Important: This brief presents market data to help evaluate territory fit. It is not a financial projection or earnings claim, and nothing here guarantees revenue or profit.
